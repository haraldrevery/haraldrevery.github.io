What to check on this page

  • Exactly ONE <h1> — the first hero's. Every later hero uses <h2> on purpose; a real page has one hero, and one title.
  • Exactly ONE back link at the top (the first hero's), and one at the bottom, which post_body.njk always emits.
  • Both cover heroes in BOTH colour schemes. Their text colour is pinned to the scrim, not to the theme, so this is where it goes wrong.
  • The scroll prompt fades in and drops the page to just under its own hero, not to the top of the next one.
  • The inlined svgs flip colour with the scheme; the linked one on the other stress page does not, and that is the difference between the two SVG modes.
